Sensors

Set mounting measurements for sensors registered on the Devices page. Saving does not reconfigure physical devices.

Physical sensors

Select a sensor and click the room plan to place it. All distances are in mm.

Yaw 0° faces from FRONT REFERENCE WALL towards BACK WALL. Positive angles turn counterclockwise on the plan. Pitch tilts up/down; roll rotates around the facing direction. The 2D map uses yaw; pitch and roll are stored for later 3D geometry.
